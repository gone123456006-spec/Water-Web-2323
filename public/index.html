Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. Find everything you need to know about our water service.

01 Is your water safe for children and seniors? +

Yes. Our water is processed through RO, UV and Ozone and tested regularly so it is safe for all age groups when consumed as part of a balanced diet.

02 Do you charge a deposit for 20L jars? +

Yes, a one‑time refundable deposit is taken per jar. You get it back when you return all jars in usable condition.

03 What are your regular delivery timings? +

Standard deliveries are done between 7:00 AM – 8:00 PM. For urgent requirements you can call or WhatsApp us directly.

04 Do you supply water for offices, shops and events? +

Yes. Use the Business / Bulk Enquiry form below or message us on WhatsApp with your approximate daily or event requirement.

05 How do I place an order? +

You can order by calling us at +91 8974668938, sending a WhatsApp message, or using the contact form on this website. We'll confirm your order and delivery time.

06 What is the minimum order quantity? +

For home delivery, minimum order is 1 jar. For bulk orders (offices, events), please contact us for special pricing and minimum quantities.

07 Is the water tested and certified? +

Yes. Our water undergoes regular lab testing and meets BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) guidelines. We maintain strict quality control at every step.

08 Do you offer subscription or monthly plans? +

Yes, we offer flexible subscription plans for regular customers. Contact us to discuss daily, weekly, or monthly delivery schedules with special pricing.
